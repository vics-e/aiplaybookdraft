AI Output Review Checklist

The four output checks:

Every AI output should follow a simple review process.

1. Accuracy

Are the facts and figures correct?

2. Completeness

Is anything missing?

3. Professional tone

Is the wording appropriate and clear?

4. Compliance and ethics

Does the output align with professional standards, firm policies, and confidentiality rules?

Pattern guidance

Best used for
Independent checklist items, FAQs, definitions, or optional supporting detail.
Avoid when
Every answer is essential to understand the whole or must remain visible.
Responsive
Native disclosure rows use the full width at every breakpoint.

Comparison grid

A direct side-by-side boundary that makes policy distinctions visible without serial cards.

The Minimum AI Policy

Approved and restricted uses

Every firm should have a simple, clear policy for AI use.

Approved uses

AI may be used for:

  • Drafting communications
  • Summarising notes
  • Preparing explanations
  • Internal checklists
  • And other low-risk tasks

Restricted uses

AI must not be used for:

  • Final client outputs without review
  • High-judgement decisions
  • Unapproved workflows

Pattern guidance

Best used for
Two to four categories with parallel criteria, contrasts, or boundaries.
Avoid when
Cells contain long narratives or categories have little in common.
Responsive
Columns stack in reading order with their relationship retained by shared framing.

Timeline / process strip

A time-bound journey where cadence, progression, and a goal at each phase matter more than equal-sized cards.

The 90-Day AI Adoption Plan

Your roadmap from foundations to your first agent

Three phases clarify the progression without turning every action into a separate tile.

Days 1–30

Build the Foundations

Establish policy, tools, and team understanding.

Goal: Every team member understands what AI is allowed, what's prohibited, and how to use it safely.

Days 31–60

Move from Prompts to Workflows

Standardise AI use and build repeatable processes.

Goal: AI use is consistent across the team, embedded into defined workflows, and creating measurable capacity.

Days 61–90

Introduce Your First Agent

Deploy your first goal-driven AI workflow.

Goal: Your first agent is live, monitored, and delivering measurable results with full professional oversight.

Pattern guidance

Best used for
Roadmaps, maturity progressions, milestones, and time-based processes.
Avoid when
Items have no chronological or causal relationship.
Responsive
The horizontal strip becomes a vertical path with a clear top-to-bottom sequence.

Decision summary

Recommended mapping

Choose by content intent, not by the number of items available. This keeps patterns distinct and prevents another visual monoculture.

Content intentRecommended treatmentWhy it fits
Teach a short ordered frameworkHorizontal stepsPreserves sequence while giving each part focused space.
Browse several peer examplesSelector + detailKeeps one rich example visible without a long repeated stack.
Scan optional or independent detailAccordionFront-loads labels and lets readers open only what they need.
Distinguish categories or boundariesComparison gridSupports direct visual comparison across parallel criteria.
Explain progression over timeTimeline / process stripMakes direction, cadence, and milestones explicit.
