  - heading "The AI Impact Pricing Exercise" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Mapping the financial impact of AI across your services
  - paragraph: Before restructuring pricing, it helps to understand exactly where AI creates efficiency in your firm—and what that efficiency is worth. This exercise maps the time, cost, and pricing implications of AI across your key services.
  - paragraph: "For each service, you need to understand four things: how long it takes today, how long it will take with AI assistance, what human value remains after AI does its work, and what the pricing implication is."
  - heading "How to Complete the Exercise:" [level=4]
  - paragraph: "For each service or task type in your firm: 1. Estimate the current time to complete (e.g., 6 hours for a standard set of accounts) 2. Estimate the time with AI assistance (e.g., 3.5 hours) 3. Identify what human activity remains and why it matters (e.g., review, judgement, client conversation) 4. Decide the pricing implication: maintain fee (improve margin), increase fee (if value increases), or restructure (move to fixed/tiered model) This gives you a clear picture of where AI improves economics and where pricing changes are needed."
  - complementary "Learning":
    - paragraph: The goal is not to lower prices because AI is faster. It is to understand where margin improves, where you can invest more advisory time, and where pricing needs to evolve.
