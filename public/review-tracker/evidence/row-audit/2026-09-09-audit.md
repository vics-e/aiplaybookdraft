## Counting rules

- Checked = Passed/Already evidenced with full supporting evidence, plus Failed.
- A partial attempt is not counted as completed or checked in this deliberately strict metric.
- Not applicable and Needs Sage or QA are separate dispositions.
- A failed row may be established by a reproducible counterexample without exhausting every passing case.
- Passing a tool-run requirement (manual-37/38) means the specified audit was run and findings recorded, not performance/accessibility conformance.
